# Visual Brief — v0.1.0 Article Visuals

## Article

**Title**: Why Local Observability Comes Before Automation

**Subtitle**: A Stage 1 checkpoint for building governed identity, execution, and continuity before external action.

## Article Summary

The article explains why Signal Agent v0.1.0 begins with system coherence and local spine observability before any external action. It frames the release as a bounded Stage 1 checkpoint: repo-grounded documentation, a local-only append-only spine/platform/metric observability layer, deterministic summaries, under-tracked detection, GitHub release, Zenodo archive, and archive record.

The article is downstream from the repository, release, and archive. It interprets the artifact chain; it does not create authority by itself.

## Tone

- Clear
- Serious
- Technical
- Boundary-disciplined
- Non-promotional
- Evidence-first
- Calm rather than dramatic

## Core Themes

- System coherence before scale
- Observation before action
- Identity-aligned operational lanes
- Signal becoming continuity
- Artifact-first publication
- Local append-only state
- Bounded governance
- Public explanation as a downstream artifact

## Visual Goals

- Help readers feel the order of the system: evidence, implementation, documentation, release, archive, article, social downflow, signal capture.
- Make "local observability before automation" visually legible.
- Show spines as structured identity-aligned lanes, not separate brands or autonomous personas.
- Reinforce containment and restraint rather than speed or spectacle.
- Give social posts a visual anchor without implying external platform automation.

## Non-Claims

Visuals must not imply:

- external platform ingestion
- scraping
- API automation
- posting automation
- messaging automation
- autonomous metric collection
- external actions
- dashboard integration
- live social monitoring
- autonomous cross-platform intelligence

2. **Supporting Concept Visual: Spines**
   - Explains identity-aligned operational lanes.
   - Shows Reflective Spine and Governance Spine emerging from one coherent source.

3. **Supporting Concept Visual: Bounded Publication Chain**
   - Shows the artifact-first chain from repo evidence through archive record to article and social downflow.

4. **Social Quote Cards**
   - Short, restrained graphics for LinkedIn, X/Threads, and Facebook derivatives.
   - Uses key lines from the article without adding claims.

## Style Guidance

- Serious, clean, conceptual, systems-oriented.
- Use restrained structure: grids, paths, lanes, ledgers, labels, containment boundaries.
- Prefer calm contrast and readable hierarchy over dramatic glow or sci-fi styling.
- Avoid cartoonish characters, robots, fake dashboards, network-control rooms, and platform logos.
- Avoid visuals that suggest active external integrations, live scraping, or automated posting.
- Make the images feel like an artifact system, not a product advertisement.
