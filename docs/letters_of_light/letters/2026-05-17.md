Some weeks do not ask us to solve everything. They ask us to take the next faithful step without surrendering our peace.

Peace is not denial. Peace does not pretend pain is small, or consequences are easy, or waiting is simple. Peace is the quiet strength that says, "I can do the next right thing without becoming ruled by fear."

Jesus speaks of a peace that is given, not manufactured. That matters because many of us have tried to build peace out of control. We wait to feel calm until the answer comes, the door opens, the apology arrives, the pressure lifts, or the plan becomes clear.

But the peace of God can meet us before the full answer does.

This week, do not confuse peace with passivity. Seeking peace may require an apology. It may require silence when a sharp answer would feel easier. It may require a boundary. It may require telling the truth gently. It may require choosing the next small good thing while your emotions are still catching up.

You do not have to carry the whole road today. Carry the next step with a clean heart.

Let peace be more than a feeling. Let it become a practice: pause before reacting, pray before deciding, listen before defending, and repair what you can repair.

The light for the whole path may not be visible yet. But there is enough light for the next step.
