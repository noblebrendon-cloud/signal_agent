# Governed Authoring Studio V1A Privacy Note

## 1. Status

This is a plain-language V1A privacy note.

It is not a full legal privacy policy.

A formal privacy policy is required before a public hosted beta or public app
launch.

## 2. What V1A Is

V1A is a concierge-assisted thin UI for Governed Authoring Studio.

That means the experience may look like a product interface, but a human
operator may manually review submitted material to help prepare the output
packet.

The V1A output packet may include:

- clarified direction
- artifact spine
- first draft section
- review gate summary
- exportable text or markdown
- suggested next step

## 3. What You May Submit

You may submit:

- project title
- artifact type
- notes/fragments/source material
- desired output
- target audience
- tone/style preferences
- constraints
- examples/references
- contact/delivery information

Submit only material you are comfortable having reviewed for this V1A workflow.

## 4. What You Should Not Submit

Do not submit:

- passwords
- financial account numbers
- Social Security numbers
- private medical records
- legal case documents unless explicitly requested and safely handled
- confidential third-party data
- anything you do not want a human operator to see during V1A
- external platform credentials
- private material belonging to someone else without permission

If sensitive details are not necessary for the artifact, leave them out or
generalize them.

## 5. How Your Material Is Used

Submitted material is used to create:

- clarified direction
- artifact spine
- first draft section
- review gate summary
- exportable output packet

Submitted material should not be used for public marketing, training examples,
or publication without explicit permission.

The purpose of V1A is to help turn your submitted source material into a
structured, reviewable draft artifact.

## 6. Manual Operator Access

Because V1A is concierge-assisted, a human operator may see submitted source
material and generated outputs.

This must be disclosed before intake submission.

Operator access is used to:

- check intake completeness
- clarify ambiguity
- prepare the artifact spine
- draft one section
- run the review gate
- prepare the output packet
- record limited product-learning notes

Operator access should be limited to what is needed to complete the V1A
workflow.

## 7. Source and Output Separation

Original source material and generated output should remain separate.

Generated output should not silently overwrite source material.

You should be able to identify:

- what you submitted
- what was clarified
- what was generated
- what was reviewed
- what was exported

This separation protects user trust and keeps the workflow from becoming a
black box.

## 8. Private by Default

Projects and submitted materials are private by default.

No public sharing happens without user approval.

V1A outputs should not be used as public examples, marketing copy, demos, or
training material without explicit permission.

## 9. Export and Return of Work

You receive an output packet after a V1A run.

The packet may include:

- structured artifact spine
- first draft section
- review summary
- plain text export
- markdown export
- suggested next step

You should be able to keep and use your returned work.

## 10. Deletion / Archive Request

During V1A, users may request deletion or archive of submitted materials and
outputs according to the current operating policy.

A more formal retention/deletion policy must be defined before public beta.

Until that policy exists, V1A should keep data handling narrow, explicit, and
manual.

## 11. Data Minimization

Only information needed to complete the V1A workflow should be collected.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive details.

V1A should not collect:

- unnecessary demographics
- social graph
- contacts
- location
- external account tokens
- platform posting credentials
- private analytics beyond product usage
- sensitive profile categories unrelated to the artifact

The less unnecessary private material collected, the easier it is to protect.

## 12. AI / Tool Use Disclosure

V1A may use AI-assisted drafting or review tools under operator supervision.

Sensitive material should not be pasted into public tools without appropriate
care and user disclosure.

The operator should avoid exposing unnecessary private material.

AI/tool use should support the artifact workflow. It should not replace the
user's judgment, authorship, or approval.

## 13. No Professional Advice

The V1A output is creative and structural assistance.

It is not:

- legal advice
- medical advice
- financial advice
- tax advice
- therapeutic advice
- professional certification or compliance review

If your artifact depends on professional advice, consult a qualified
professional before relying on the output.

## 14. User Control

The user controls:

- whether to submit material
- whether to approve the generated direction/spine
- whether to continue after the first output
- whether to request deletion/archive
- whether anything is shared publicly

V1A should support user agency. It should not pressure the user to continue,
publish, or expose private work.

## 15. Plain-Language Intake Consent Block

Copy-safe intake consent block:

```text
I understand that Governed Authoring Studio V1A is concierge-assisted. A human
operator may review my submitted material to prepare my output packet. I will
not submit passwords, financial account numbers, medical records, legal
documents, confidential third-party data, or anything I do not want reviewed by
an operator. My material will remain private by default and will not be shared
publicly without my approval.
```

Recommended required checkboxes:

- I understand V1A is concierge-assisted.
- I understand a human operator may review my submitted material.
- I understand my material is private by default.
- I understand I should not submit highly sensitive material unless explicitly
  requested and safely handled.
- I understand my material will not be shared publicly without my approval.

## 16. Future Public Beta Requirements

Before public beta, the product must have:

- formal privacy policy
- terms of use
- retention/deletion policy
- security review
- access-control model
- user export process
- incident/contact process
- AI/tool-use disclosure
- age/minor policy if applicable
- documented operator access rules
- documented data retention boundaries

The V1A privacy note is not enough for public beta.

## 17. Operator Checklist

Before processing V1A material, confirm:

- manual access was disclosed
- user acknowledged operator-assisted review
- only needed material was collected
- source and output remain separate
- unnecessary sensitive exposure is avoided
- no public sharing occurs without approval
- deletion/archive requests are honored under policy
- user content is not used in marketing without permission
- private material is not pasted into public tools without appropriate care
- operator notes are minimal and workflow-relevant

Privacy is part of the product experience. It should be handled with the same
care as the artifact itself.
