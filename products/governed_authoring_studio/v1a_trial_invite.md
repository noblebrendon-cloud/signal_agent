# Governed Authoring Studio V1A Trial Invite

Status: V1A trial invitation draft

## 1. Purpose

This document provides reusable invitation copy for the first 5-10 Governed
Authoring Studio V1A trial participants.

The invite should be short, human, non-salesy, and clear about the early state
of the product.

## 2. Core Message

```text
I am testing a guided workflow that helps people turn scattered notes or foggy
ideas into a clear project direction, a structured artifact spine, one usable
draft section, a review summary, and next steps.
```

## 3. Boundaries To Preserve

Every invite should make clear:

- this is not a public app yet
- this is a concierge-assisted test
- a human operator may review submitted material
- participants should not submit anything highly sensitive
- the goal is to test whether the workflow helps people get unstuck
- there is no obligation to continue
- the participant will be asked for feedback

## 4. Short Invite

```text
I am testing an early version of Governed Authoring Studio, a guided workflow
for people with serious unfinished ideas.

The trial is simple: you share rough notes, fragments, or a foggy project idea.
I use the workflow to turn that material into a clear direction, a structured
artifact spine, one draft section, a review summary, and suggested next steps.

This is not a public app yet. It is concierge-assisted, which means a human
operator may review what you submit. Please do not send passwords, financial
details, medical records, legal documents, confidential third-party material, or
anything you would not want reviewed during this test.

The goal is to learn whether this helps people get unstuck and make real
progress on work that has been sitting in fragments.

Would you be open to being one of the first 5-10 test users?
```

## 5. Warmer Personal Invite

```text
I am testing a small early workflow that I think might fit the kind of work you
do.

It is called Governed Authoring Studio. The idea is to help people with serious
unfinished thoughts turn messy notes or fragments into something more usable:
a clear direction, a structured spine, one draft section, a review summary, and
a next step.

This is not a public app yet. It is a concierge-assisted test, so a human
operator may review submitted material to prepare the return packet. Please do
not submit anything highly sensitive or anything you would not want reviewed in
that context.

I am looking for 5-10 early testers who have a real stuck idea: a book, essay
series, course outline, framework, or teaching concept that has not quite found
its shape yet.

No pressure to publish or continue. I mostly want to know whether the workflow
helps you get clearer and unstuck.

Would you want to try it?
```

## 6. Direct Professional Invite

```text
I am running a small V1A trial for Governed Authoring Studio.

It is a concierge-assisted workflow for turning rough notes or unfinished ideas
into:

- a clarified project direction
- a structured artifact spine
- one usable draft section
- a review summary
- suggested next steps

This is not a public SaaS app yet. A human operator may review submitted
material during the test, so participants should not submit highly sensitive
information or confidential third-party material.

I am testing with 5-10 people to see whether the workflow helps users make real
progress on stuck creative or intellectual projects.

If you have an unfinished book idea, essay series, course outline, framework,
or teaching artifact, I would be glad to include you in the test.
```

## 7. What Participants Receive

Participants receive:

- clarified direction
- artifact spine
- one first draft section
- review gate summary
- plain text or markdown export
- suggested next step

They do not receive:

- a finished full book/course/series
- publication-ready output
- legal, medical, financial, or professional advice
- campaign automation
- direct publishing
- a full hosted app account

## 8. Who To Invite First

Best first participants:

- people with real unfinished ideas
- people who can give honest feedback
- people who are not expecting a complete done-for-you service
- writers
- educators
- founders
- researchers
- operators
- coaches
- artists
- builders

Avoid first:

- people needing regulated professional advice
- people who mainly want a social campaign
- people who need team collaboration
- people who are not comfortable with operator-assisted review
- people with highly sensitive material

## 9. Follow-Up After Yes

```text
Great. I will send you a short intake form.

You can paste messy notes, fragments, or a rough idea. It does not need to be
organized. The only important boundary is: do not submit anything highly
sensitive or anything you would not want reviewed by a human operator during
this early test.

After intake, you will receive a structured spine, one draft section, a review
summary, and a suggested next step.
```

## 10. Follow-Up After Delivery

Ask:

- Did the spine reflect your idea?
- Did the first draft section feel useful?
- Did the draft sound connected to your material?
- Was the review summary specific enough?
- Did you know what to do next?
- Did anything feel too generic?
- Did the privacy/operator-access note feel clear?
- Would you pay to continue this project?
- Would you run another artifact through this workflow?

## 11. Consent Reminder

Before sending the intake form, include:

```text
One important note: V1A is concierge-assisted. A human operator may review your
submitted material to prepare the output packet. Please do not submit passwords,
financial account numbers, medical records, legal documents, confidential
third-party material, or anything you do not want reviewed during this test.
```
