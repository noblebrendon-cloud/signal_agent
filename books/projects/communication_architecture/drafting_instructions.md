# Drafting Instructions

## Tone

- Analytical
- Reflective
- Systems-oriented
- Accessible but dense

## Exclusions

- Do not write self-help.
- Do not write guru-style advice.
- Do not present manipulative persuasion tactics as the point of the book.
- Do not become conspiratorial.
- Do not use fake neuroscience.
- Do not treat language as deterministic mind control.

## Drafting Frame

- Treat language as pressure, terrain, and architecture.
- Keep human agency visible even while describing influence systems.
- Distinguish persuasion, resonance, governance, coercion, and manipulation.
- Prefer procedural legitimacy and incentive analysis over moral panic.
- Connect system-level claims to ordinary communicative experience without
  turning the book into a tactics manual.
- Preserve uncertainty where empirical support or normative judgment would need
  later sourcing.

## Current Bookgen Constraint

The current renderer consumes the YAML render spec, not this authoring packet.
Draft chapter prose should replace the explicit placeholders in
`books/specs/communication_architecture.yaml` until a future project-to-spec
compiler exists.
