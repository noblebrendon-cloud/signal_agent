Governed Communication

Chapter source: 15_governed_communication.md

Reviewed
Governed communication is practical structure.

Coherence needs structure, not slogans. A system can praise truth while rewarding speed. It can praise agency while designing for dependency. It can praise openness while hiding procedure. Governed communication is the attempt to make the structure match the claim.

Bounded influence, clarification pathways, visible procedure, and non-extractive design make influence answerable while preserving agency.

Continuity Rule

Preserve coherence without enforcing sameness.

Invariant

Agency is supported by architecture, not replaced by it.
