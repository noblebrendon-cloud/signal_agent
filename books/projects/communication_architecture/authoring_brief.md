## Core Thesis

Human communication is not merely informational. It is architectural. Language
shapes perception, identity, legitimacy, memory, behavior, and social reality.
Modern communication systems increasingly optimize emotional velocity rather
than coherence or truth. The deeper problem is not simply misinformation, but
governance failure within discourse systems themselves.

## Strategic Purpose

This book should become a canonical authorship artifact supporting larger system
work around governed AI, deterministic orchestration, discourse legitimacy,
coherence, and human agency under acceleration. It should establish conceptual
legitimacy in the author's own words and under the author's own name.

## Scope

The book is short-to-medium length and analytical. It should connect individual
communication mechanics to the discourse systems that reward, deform, or govern
them. Its center of gravity is legitimate communication architecture under
pressure, not a catalog of persuasion tricks.
